    What drove errors

    Machines whose exact model had never sold before (93 sales) scored 0.395; models with 100+ earlier sales, 0.216. Unknown year made: 0.317. Across Jan to Apr 2012 the estimates ran 1% to 5% low on average: prices moved after the training data ended and trees can't extrapolate a trend.

    Against the course

    The course's random forest reaches 0.247 on the same 2012 sales, but its settings were picked by scoring them on those sales. Gavel picked everything on late-2011 sales first and opened 2012 once: 0.228.

    What didn't work

    Ordering category codes by average price, a standard trick, did worse than plain alphabetical codes (0.246 against 0.230 on the tuning window): the codes leak each category's own prices into training.
